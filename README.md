# griddle-legal
Griddle Privacy Policy
Effective 26 October 2026

Griddle ("we", "us") is a social discovery service based in Bengaluru, India. It helps people plan outings and find places to eat and things to do. This policy explains what information we handle when you message the Griddle Instagram account (@griddle.app) and how you can ask us to delete it.

What we collect
Your messages to @griddle.app, including text and links you send, such as a place name or an Instagram reel.
Your Instagram-scoped ID (a number Instagram gives us so we can reply to you) and, where Instagram provides it, your public profile name and username.
Our replies and a log of the places we matched to your message.
We do not collect your Instagram password, your contacts, your payment details or your precise location.

How we use it
To understand your message, find the place or experience you mean, and reply.
To improve how accurately we match places and to fix mistakes in our place data.
To prevent spam and abuse of the service.
We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it
Your message text may be processed by service providers that help us run the service: Meta (which operates Instagram), our cloud hosting and spreadsheet providers, and an AI language model provider used to read your message. These providers only receive what is needed to do that job.

How long we keep it
We keep message logs for up to 12 months, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your choices and data deletion
You can ask us at any time to delete the messages and ID we hold about you. To do so, email griddev123@gmail.com from any address with the subject "Delete my data" and include your Instagram username. We will delete the data and confirm by reply within 30 days. You can also stop at any time by not messaging the account or by deleting the conversation in Instagram.

Children
Griddle is not intended for people under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes
If we change this policy we will update the date above.
